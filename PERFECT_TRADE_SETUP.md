# 🎯 PERFECT TRADE SETUP - QUICK REFERENCE

## ✅ ENTRY REQUIREMENTS (ALL MUST BE TRUE)

### 1. CANDLE DIRECTION (CRITICAL - NO EXCEPTIONS)
- **LONG:** GREEN candle ONLY (close > open)
- **SHORT:** RED candle ONLY (close < open)
- **Candle Body:** ≥15% of ATR (no tiny dojis)

### 2. TREND CONFIRMATION (STRONG TREND ONLY)
- **ADX:** ≥30 (preferably rising)
- **Consecutive Bars:** 3+ same direction
- **Hull MA:** All aligned (Main + Fast same direction)
- **SuperTrend:** Confirming (1 for LONG, -1 for SHORT)
- **Price vs EMA21:** Beyond EMA (above for LONG, below for SHORT)

### 3. MOMENTUM CONFIRMATION
- **Volume:** ≥1.5× average (strong participation)
- **RSI:** 40-65 (not overbought/oversold)
- **Stochastic:** 25-75 (not extreme)

### 4. VOLATILITY CONFIRMATION
- **ATR:** Expanding (≥1.1× SMA)
- **BB Width:** ≥0.020 (not tight consolidation)

### 5. MARKET STATE
- **NOT Choppy:** Pass 2/3 checks:
  - ADX ≥25
  - ATR ≥0.8× average
  - BB width ≥0.015
- **Quality Score:** ≥70/100

### 6. SESSION & TIMING
- **NY Session:** 9:30 AM - 4:00 PM ET (optimal)
- **Not No-Trade Zone:** Avoid 0:00 AM - 6:00 AM ET
- **Daily Limit:** Under max trades (default 5/day)

## 💰 RISK MANAGEMENT PER TRADE

### Position Sizing (MNQ Example)
```
Account: $10,000
Risk per Trade: 1.0% = $100
Stop Distance: 42.5 points (SL + slippage buffer)
Stop Risk: 42.5 points × $2 = $85
Position Size: $100 / $85 = 1.17 → 1 contract
```

### Stop Loss Calculation
```
Entry: 24,581.75
Base Stop: 2.5 × ATR = 2.5 × 15 = 37.5 points
+ Slippage Buffer: +5 points
= Total Stop Distance: 42.5 points
Stop Loss Price: 24,581.75 - 42.5 = 24,539.25
```

### Take Profit Targets (High Win Rate Mode)
```
TP Multiplier: 0.6 (conservative)
ATR: 15 points

TP1 (50% exit): 0.6 × 1.0 × 15 = 9 points
  → 24,581.75 + 9 - 2.5 (slippage) = 24,588.25

TP2 (30% exit): 0.6 × 2.0 × 15 = 18 points
  → 24,581.75 + 18 - 1.5 (slippage) = 24,598.25

TP3 (15% exit): 0.6 × 3.5 × 15 = 31.5 points
  → 24,581.75 + 31.5 - 1.0 (slippage) = 24,612.25

TP4 (5% runner): 0.6 × 5.0 × 15 = 45 points
  → 24,581.75 + 45 = 24,626.75
```

### Trailing Stop Activation
```
Step 1: Profit reaches 15 points (1.0 ATR)
  → Move stop to breakeven + slippage buffer
  → New stop: 24,581.75 + 5 = 24,586.75

Step 2: Profit reaches 30 points (2.0 ATR)
  → Activate tight trailing: 0.3 ATR = 4.5 points
  → Trail at: Current price - 4.5 points

Step 3: Trail never moves backwards
  → Only tightens as price moves up
  → Locks in profits automatically
```
